ClassCompass is a platform designed to streamline grade monitoring and provide real-time updates on student performance. As students, we all value learning environments that are efficient, supportive, and stress-free. By incorporating predictive analysis, ClassCompass helps identify students who may be struggling early on, allowing teachers to intervene before small issues become major setbacks. Students are automatically grouped into three categories based on their grades: good standing, needs attention, and struggling. With these classifications, instructors can easily recognize who is struggling and offer a helping hand.  

Another key feature of ClassCompass is its use of data visualizations. Raw numbers can be difficult to interpret, but visual graphs make trends easy to understand at a glance. Teachers can quickly see class-wide performance, identify which topics were the most challenging, and adjust their teaching strategies accordingly.  

What truly sets ClassCompass apart is its interactive feedback forum. Learning is a two-way process: teachers guide students, but students also provide valuable insight into how they learn best. Through this forum, students can share feedback such as, "Could you slow down on this topic? Many of us are struggling," or "The exams don't align with the lectures, could we revisit that?" This fosters open communication, helping instructors refine their teaching and ensuring students feel heard.  

ClassCompass also provides an advanced settings page, allowing users to personalize their experience. Teachers can toggle dark mode, upload or remove background images, and customize grade boundaries (e.g., what percentage qualifies as an A, A-, B, etc.).  

Because of time and resource limitations, some features are still in development. Currently, the platform only includes the instructor view. In the future, we plan to create dedicated profiles for students and parents. We also aim to expand the settings page to support full language switching, additional grading display options (letters, percentages, GPA, or all three), and customizable grading policies such as dropping the lowest score or adjusting category weights for homework, labs, tests, and projects. We hope all these implementations can be activated easily by the instructor, thus reducing stress and workload for them. A built-in grade/GPA calculator is also on the roadmap. Teachers will be able to enable or disable the calculator and the feedback forum depending on their preferences. Another major planned feature is the ability to download or export data for increased accessibility.  

Overall, ClassCompass is an application designed to make academic life easier for both instructors and students. Even in its current stage, the platform brings together key features commonly found in systems like LMS Blackboard, while adding powerful key new capabilities such as intuitive data visualizations and an interactive feedback forum, all in one unified space. As we continue developing our planned enhancements, ClassCompass aims to become the single platform instructors, students, and even parents can rely on for every academic need. By combining performance tracking, seamless communication, customization options, and smart data analytics, ClassCompass creates a more connected, supportive, and effective educational experience for everyone involved.

Start using ClassCompass today and discover a smarter, more connected way to teach and learn!
